MODULE 01 · 6 MINUTES

What is Snowflake?

Imagine a library. Your data stays on shelves. When you need answers, Snowflake gives you a temporary team of librarians. That team is called a virtual warehouse.

The important idea
Snowflake separates storage (your data) from compute (the power used to query it). You can grow or pause each independently.
USE DATABASE SALES_DB;
SELECT * FROM ORDERS LIMIT 10;

🧠 Memory hook

Warehouse = workers, not shelves. It is compute power. Turn workers off when no one is querying to save credits.
